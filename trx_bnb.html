How to Use

  1. Enter both the Sender and Receiver wallet addresses correctly
  2. Send TRX to the displayed deposit address
  3. Receive BNB (BSC) automatically (usually within 5 minutes depending on network conditions)
  4. Note: To prevent automated attacks and speculative swaps, any transaction value outside the range of 2 – 10 USDT will be automatically refunded (minus a fee for processing).
  5. Note: If the gas fee for the refunded token temporarily spikes above 0.5 USDT, the swap fee increases to 1 USDT. If the gas fee exceeds 1.0 USDT, the full amount will be refunded to the sender.
